# Sound credits

Recorded sound effects (explosions, gunshots, impacts, woosh, machine-gun burst) are from the
**Superpowers Western FPS 2D asset pack** by Pixel-boy / Sparklin Labs, released under
**CC0 1.0** (public domain): https://github.com/sparklinlabs/superpowers-asset-packs

Engines, ocean, wind, construction, factory, radar and interface sounds are synthesized in
code (`src/audio/`).

## Music

`zarathustra.mp3`: *Also sprach Zarathustra*, Op. 30, opening ("Sunrise"), by Richard Strauss
(composition in the public domain). Recording conducted by **Philip Milman** (PM Music), from
*Lud and Schlatt's Musical Emporium*, licensed **CC BY 3.0**
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Changes: re-encoded to 128 kbit/s MP3,
loudness normalised, cover art and tags removed. Source file:
https://github.com/aestuans/spectrogram/blob/main/assets/Also-Sprach-Zarathustra-PM-Music.mp3
The credit is also shown in the game's Sound panel.
